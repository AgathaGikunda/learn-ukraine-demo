Data methodology — MoES route registry + hromada declaration Unit of measurement: MoES-registered bus route. Each route serves one or more schools in a hromada. Hromada education officers submit a GEMS declaration form confirming the route is active, which schools it serves, how many students are on the manifest, whether subvention has been received, and whether the bus is disability-accessible. Routes are then matched against the MoES national registry. IVA cross-checks a sample of declared routes against school enrolment registers and Transport Ministry records.
